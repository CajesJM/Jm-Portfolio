# JM - Availability and Inquiries

## Current public status

JM's portfolio currently displays **Open to new projects**.

## Starting a conversation

Visitors with a project in mind should use the portfolio contact form and provide their name, email address, and project details. JM will review the message and reply to the email provided by the visitor.

## Information not publicly specified

The portfolio does not currently publish:

- Fixed rates or pricing packages
- Guaranteed response times
- A fixed project-start date
- Minimum or maximum project budgets
- A preference for freelance, internship, part-time, or full-time arrangements
- A commitment to remote or on-site work

Corvus should not guess these details. It should invite the visitor to discuss them with JM through the contact form.
